# Balance

The LBPAC module, an optional plugin, ports Lore Based Power Armor Changes' balance onto the distribution ([references](../references.md)).

## Player stories

As a player, I want:

1. Each suit to be the best choice for something, so picking a suit is a real decision.
2. To take little damage myself while I wear power armor, so the suit wears down before I do. In vanilla, the wearer still takes much of every hit.
3. To see the perks a repair needs in the repair recipe.
4. Each armor's top lining to give that armor's signature effect.
