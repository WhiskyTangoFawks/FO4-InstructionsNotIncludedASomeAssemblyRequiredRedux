# Distribution

Power armor is something the player earns, not something the world hands over ([principles](../principles.md), Earned, not given).

## Player stories

As a player, I want:

1. To earn my power armor. A suit is a project I build up piece by piece across a playthrough, and climbing into a full set is a moment I worked for.
2. Exploring to pay off. Power armor turns up where the world would have left it, so reading a place and searching it thoroughly is how I find it.
3. The early game to stay dangerous. The opening hours give me a taste of power armor, not a suit.
4. Each suit to be as rare as its story makes it, so finding a rare piece is an event, and the rarest suit is a hunt of its own.
5. The Brotherhood's armor to be the Brotherhood's. Joining them is how I get it.
6. A place that once held a free suit to still reward the trip, with supplies for the fights ahead.
7. Enemies in power armor to stay rare, memorable threats, and a fight I won to stay won.
8. A showpiece suit to be a curiosity I restore with effort, never a shortcut to the top.

## Modlist author stories

As a modlist author, I want:

1. The power armor that other mods place to follow the same scarcity, as far as it can without a patch.
2. The mod to sit beside previs mods without breaking their cells.
3. To know what a patch for a mod that adds power armor must do: bring its placements into the scarcity.
4. To add the mod to a game in progress, knowing that only cells not yet visited change.
