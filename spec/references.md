# References

The mods this one builds on, and the ones it was chosen over.

## Some Assembly Required

Built on, for distribution.

- Places suits the original missed.
- Swaps the sets that other mods place, through the Base Object Swapper file.
- Gives Danse a T-60 with no Brotherhood paint after Blind Betrayal.
- Makes the Vim, Nuka-Cola and Nuka Quantum sets standard T-51 with display linings.

## Lore Based Power Armor Changes Redux

Built on, for balance: the LBPAC module ports its balance and keeps Some Assembly Required's distribution. It is this mod's author's redux of Lore Based Power Armor Changes.

- Adds the Creation Club X-02 and Hellfire to the balance.
- Lowers the damage formula's minimum damage ([design](design.md), Damage formula).

## Lore Based Power Armor Changes

The balance's origin, through its redux. Its T-45 fusion core drain is rejected: with fusion cores scarce, as Lunar Fallout Overhaul makes them, the T-45 was unusable ([design](design.md), Fuel). Its distribution is rejected: it disables the suits that stand in the open and de-levels the caged ones, but power armor stays easy to find ([principles](principles.md), Earned, not given).

## Power Armor to the People

Rejected for distribution. LitR ran it with Lore Based Power Armor Changes Redux, and it put so much power armor in the world that each suit's niche stopped mattering ([principles](principles.md), Earned, not given).

## Whisky's Simple Power Armor Damage Threshold

Rejected for protection. It brings back a damage threshold for power armor through perks. A threshold can push a hit below zero damage, which heals the armor, and the mod does not run on the next-gen update ([design](design.md), Damage formula).

## Bastion

Rejected because it fundamentally changes the balance in ways that are complex to parse out with Lunar. An F4SE plugin that shields the wearer completely until a piece breaks, with armor penetration by weapon class. It shields NPCs in power armor as well, makes companions' armor unbreakable, and adds a penetration system to tune beside the balance ([design](design.md), Damage formula).

## Vanilla

Rejected for both. It hands the player a full frame in the first hour and the best suit by level 28, each suit is only slightly stronger than the one before, and power armor's resistances barely change what the wearer takes.

## Raider Power Armor Chop-Shop and Children of Atom Power Armor

This mod replaces each one's plugin with its own ([architecture](architecture.md)).
