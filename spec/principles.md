# Principles

Order is rank: when two principles pull apart, the higher one wins.

## Earned, not given
A suit of power armor is an achievement the player works toward across a playthrough. Power armor handed out freely stops meaning anything, however well it is balanced.

## The world is plausible
What the player finds, and what each suit can do, is what two centuries after the war would leave: who built a suit, when, and for what decides its strengths and costs. A choice that serves fun against the setting is wrong.

## Every suit has a niche
Progression is not a line. Each suit is the best choice for something, and pays for it somewhere else, so no suit makes another obsolete. A suit that is better than another at everything has no reason for the weaker one to exist.

## The wearer is a walking tank
Inside a suit, the player survives what would kill them outside it. That power is the payoff for earning the suit.

## Compatible by default
The mod changes what it must and nothing more, so it sits in a large modlist.
