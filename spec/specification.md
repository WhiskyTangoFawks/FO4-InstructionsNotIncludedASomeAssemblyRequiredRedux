# Instructions Not Included - A Some Assembly Required Redux

A redux of Some Assembly Required: power armor is found piece by piece, in the places that make sense for it, and never handed over as a full frame. An optional module ports Lore Based Power Armor Changes' balance onto that distribution, so each suit has a niche of its own and its wearer is a walking tank. Nexus mod 103268.

- [Distribution](specification/distribution.md): where power armor is, and what stands where it was.
- [Balance](specification/balance.md): the LBPAC module, how each armor plays.
